A small squared label (like a 判子 stamp) for metadata: 業種, 死因, 年代. Not pill-shaped — squared corners keep the editorial tone.

```jsx
<Tag variant="default">飲食</Tag>
<Tag variant="accent">資金繰り</Tag>
<Tag variant="solid">2014 没</Tag>
```

Variants: `default` (ink hairline), `solid` (ink fill), `accent` (vermilion hairline — use for 死因/cause), `seal` (vermilion fill), `muted`. Keep labels short.
